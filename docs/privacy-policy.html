VibeCheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application VibeCheck (package com.dbrckk.vibecheck), published by dbrckk.

Summary

VibeCheck is designed to work primarily offline. The current V1 does not require an account, does not display advertising, and does not include analytics or behavioral tracking.

Data processed on your device

VibeCheck may store game-related information locally on your device, such as player names or nicknames, saved groups, local game statistics, appearance preferences, onboarding state, question history, and current game state. This information is used to provide the app's features and is not sent by VibeCheck to a developer-operated server.

Sharing

When you choose to share a result or challenge, VibeCheck creates the content locally and opens Android's system sharing interface. The destination app or service you choose may process the shared content under its own privacy policy. VibeCheck does not automatically upload shared content.

Public-profile simulations

Solo Party can generate fictional entertainment responses associated with public-profile personas. These responses are generated for the game and do not come from those people or represent their real opinions.

Advertising, analytics and accounts

The current V1 does not show ads, does not require user accounts, and does not use analytics or advertising tracking SDKs during normal app use.

Network access and purchases

The core game is intended to function offline. The current V1 does not expose an in-app purchase flow. If monetization, analytics, online accounts, cloud services, or other data-processing features are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Permissions

VibeCheck does not request sensitive runtime permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, or phone access for its core features.

Data retention and deletion

Game information described above is stored locally on the device. It can be removed by clearing VibeCheck's app data or uninstalling the app. Because the current V1 has no user account or developer-operated cloud storage, there is no remote account data to delete.

Security

VibeCheck minimizes data collection by keeping game data on-device and by not using a developer-operated backend for the current V1.

Children

VibeCheck is a social entertainment game. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated server. The Google Play target-audience declaration should reflect the audience selected by the publisher in Play Console.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when VibeCheck's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions about VibeCheck, use the public project contact channel at github.com/dbrckk/vibecheck-android/issues.
