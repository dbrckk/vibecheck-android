# VibeCheck — Play Store listing EN

## Name
VibeCheck

## Short description
The party game that reveals who really knows who, together or solo.

## Full description
VibeCheck turns your phone into a quick and easy party game.

Choose a mode, add your group and answer questions to discover the trends, surprises and verdicts of the game.

### Play together
Pass the phone between players and compare your answers across several modes:
- Who of us?
- Most Likely To
- Red Flag / Green Flag
- Who knows me best?

### Play solo too
You genuinely take part in the game: you answer first, then fictional characters answer before the overall result.

Answers attributed to public profiles in Solo Party are fictional entertainment simulations. They do not come from those people and do not represent their real opinions.

### Designed to be simple
- no account required;
- core game works offline;
- player groups are stored locally on the device;
- several question styles and intensities;
- drawn pastel visual theme;
- easy-to-share results.

VibeCheck is a social entertainment game. Solo simulations must not be interpreted as real statements or opinions from public figures.
